How it works

1

Connect your accounts, securely

Link the bank accounts and UPI you already use — through India's RBI-regulated Account Aggregator framework. Consent-first and bank-grade secure. We never see your passwords.

2

Turn your spending into investing

Each week your spare change is added up and invested for you. You choose your weekly limit, your deposit day, and how much to set aside per purchase.

Stay in full control
of your money

Set your own weekly limit, skip a week whenever you like, and choose which companies you'd rather not own. Your money stays in your name, with a regulated partner — never pooled with us. Your money, your rules.
